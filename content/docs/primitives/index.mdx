Primitives are what every Videorc screen is built from: grouped lists and their rows, flush panel sections, panes with their toolbars, the status bar, kebab menus, confirm dialogs and the chrome ground under the window. Each one is the single answer for its job, so a screen composes them instead of restyling them (see [One primitive per job](/docs/concepts/design-language#one-primitive-per-job)). They're being ported from Videorc now, and each gets its page here as it lands.
