Blocks are whole pieces of Videorc's screens, composed from the [components](/docs/components) and [primitives](/docs/primitives) the way Videorc composes them. They're presentational: plain data and callbacks go in, and nothing reaches for Electron, IPC or `window.videorc`. Your app owns the state and the side effects.

## Stream Manager

Videorc's live dashboard window: the stats bar, chat beside Activity and Buddy, and the status bar. [Stream Manager](/docs/blocks/stream-manager) is the whole window; each part installs on its own too.

- [Chat Message](/docs/blocks/chat-message): one message, the big-text row.
- [Chat Toolbar](/docs/blocks/chat-toolbar): platform chips, Questions, Mentions and search.
- [Chat Composer](/docs/blocks/chat-composer): the "To:" picker, the message field and send.
- [Stream Stats Bar](/docs/blocks/stream-stats-bar): the ON AIR clock, viewers, followers and the rest.
- [Activity Feed](/docs/blocks/activity-feed): follows, subs, gifts, tips and raids.
- [Stream Manager Status Bar](/docs/blocks/stream-manager-status-bar): each platform's chat state and the window's actions.
- [Platform Filter](/docs/blocks/platform-filter) and [Chat Platform](/docs/blocks/chat-platform): the pieces the parts share.

Every block sizes itself to its container, never the viewport, so it works in a window, a split pane or a docs preview alike.
